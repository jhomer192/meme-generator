About Meme Generator

Make a meme from a template or an image on your device. Add captions, drag them into position, and adjust their size, color, alignment, and outline.

Search the template list or upload an image, edit the captions, then download your finished meme as a PNG. No account is needed. On iPhone, save the opened image to Photos.

Uploaded images are edited in your browser. The template library loads images from Imgflip, so it needs an internet connection.
